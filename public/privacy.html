Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This notice explains how 343SHOP handles information when you browse the shop, contact us, place an order request or use the optional AI shopping assistant.

Information you provide

When you prepare an order, you may enter your name, phone number, email address, area, delivery directions and optional notes. The website creates a WhatsApp message from these details. The order is not sent until you press Send in WhatsApp. 343SHOP does not collect card payments on this website.

Shopping bag

Your shopping bag is saved in your browser's local storage so it can remain available while you use the shop on the same device. You can clear this data through your browser settings.

Seller and partner applications

If you apply to join the 343SHOP seller network, we collect the shop name, location, contact person's name, phone number, product categories and any catalogue or website link you choose to provide. The application is stored in the private 343SHOP Cloudflare database so the shop owner can review it, contact you, verify the business and discuss possible partnership terms. Application details are not displayed in the public product catalogue. Submitting an application does not automatically approve a shop or publish its products. If you choose to continue the conversation on WhatsApp, the details included in that message are also handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy terms.

We use application details only for seller-network review, follow-up, fraud prevention and managing an approved business relationship. Access to full application records is restricted to the 343SHOP owner console. We retain application records while they are needed to review the request, manage a partnership or maintain appropriate business records. You may contact us through the WhatsApp contact below to request an update or deletion, subject to records we must retain for legitimate business or legal reasons.

AI shopping assistant

If you use the AI assistant, the messages you send and the recent conversation needed to answer you are transmitted to the AI provider you select: OpenAI (ChatGPT models) or Anthropic (Claude models). The AI service is processed through 343SHOP's server; API keys are not sent to your browser. 343SHOP does not intentionally save chat conversations in its own database. The selected provider processes requests under its own terms and privacy policies. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, PINs or one-time verification codes to the assistant.

Advertising and cookies

343SHOP may use Google AdSense Auto ads. When advertising is enabled and the site is approved, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to the settings and consent requirements that apply to your location. You can manage advertising and cookie settings through Google's controls and your browser settings.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare and confirm order requests, provide customer support, keep the shopping bag working, protect the website and operate optional AI and advertising features.

Sharing

Information is shared only with services needed for the feature you use: WhatsApp when you choose to send an order, the AI provider you select when you send an AI message, and Google/advertising partners when ads are enabled. We do not sell your personal information.

Data security and retention

API credentials are stored as server-side secrets and are not placed in website code. Order details may remain in your WhatsApp conversation and in communications needed to fulfil your request. Browser shopping-bag data remains on your device until you clear it. Third-party providers handle information under their own retention and security policies.

Your choices and contact

You may choose not to use the AI assistant or advertising features, clear local browser storage, or contact us to ask about information shared with the shop. For privacy questions, contact 343SHOP on WhatsApp at +255 695 981 035.

This notice is a plain-language description of current website features and is not a substitute for legal advice. It should be reviewed if the shop's services, data handling or legal obligations change.
